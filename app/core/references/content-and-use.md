# Map content and intended-use review

This English operational summary records the source review dated 2026-11-09. It concerns river/lake and administrative-area presentation/report maps. Apply requirements to the actual geography, content and intended use; do not impose a public-map submission workflow on every internal working draft.

The original research read the 2019-amended map-review provisions and 2023 public-map-content document in official PDF form. Those original-language PDFs are external references in this English edition. The source index distinguishes full reading from catalog/index access and failed service connections. Recheck current official text for an actual public-use task.

## Distinguish the kinds of requirements

Applicable law and normative documents need their own scope and provisions. Technical references depend on map type, scale and use. Project requirements—along-river names, no leader lines, a lower-left legend and consistent administrative hierarchy—are adopted design rules, not a claim of universally mandated sizes or colors.

The workflow prepares traceable material and identifies applicable issues. It does not grant surveying qualifications or replace an authority's map-review decision.

## Start with the intended use

Record whether a map is an internal draft, intended for public display/publication, or not yet assigned a use. Continue reversible data preparation, rendering and correction for drafts. A PPT file, a small image or the word “schematic” alone is not a reason to assume that no public-use requirements apply.

When the use becomes public, examine the actual final product and current requirements. Prepare concrete reviewable outputs before any authorized submission. Creating an internal draft does not itself authorize publication or communication to others; use the task's actual authorization.

The [Map Administration Regulation](https://xzfg.moj.gov.cn/front/law/detail?LawID=421) includes scope, content/source updating, administrative-boundary and review provisions. The source record identifies the relevant articles and the original review date.

## Unchanged maps and derivatives

| Final product | Review action |
|---|---|
| Unchanged public-service map provided by the competent natural-resources authority with a corresponding approval number | Check the exact map/number identity and applicable exemption under Article 6 of the map-review provisions; retain the appropriate visible number |
| Changed fill, names, elements or extent | Record a derivative and examine Article 5 and the actual public-use context; the original number does not certify the new map |
| Added thematic polygons, lines, points or text | Review the final composition; unchanged administrative geometry alone does not prove an exemption |
| Resize or crop | Check current service and authority instructions; do not invent a universal percentage-based exemption |
| Another claimed exemption | Verify the particular Article 6 conditions against the actual content and applicable law |

The [official Shanghai planning/natural-resources Q&A](https://ghzyj.sh.gov.cn/hyxw/20230222/c97d40c87fc84a0f8cd414a6bf2b8fb9.html) explains direct use and editing. Explanatory pages assist interpretation but do not replace current rules or the relevant authority's instructions.

## Approval numbers, qualification documents and sources

An exemption from review under Article 6 is different from an exemption from supplying a qualification certificate under Article 10. Neither should be inferred from the other.

Approval-number placement and validity must match the actual product and provision. Rules specifically addressing internet map services must not be generalized to every static image or PPT. In particular, a lower-left approval-number placement provision for an internet service is not a rule fixing every map legend's position.

Record title, provider, page/download URL, version, retrieval date, original approval number, changes and review status separately. An original source number must not be fabricated, borrowed or presented as a new derivative's approval. The final published content must correspond to the approved product where approval is required.

## Apply extent-dependent content requirements

The 2023 public-map-content document has particular conditions for a complete map of China, Hainan-related extents and specified insets. Apply the relevant provision to the actual map and read its exceptions. Do not extrapolate a national-map inset rule into a local Shijiu Lake map simply because both are maps.

A local map around the Jiangsu/Anhui border is outside the complete-China/Hainan examples on that geographic basis; that reasoning is not an administrative approval of a particular output. If a local map includes a national location inset, assess that inset separately. Different territorial or international extents need their applicable provisions.

## Boundaries, names and thematic attributes

Check the actual source edition, administrative changes, standard names and legally published/authorized thematic information relevant to the task. A standard background map does not verify added rivers, labels, seasonal water surfaces or flow directions. Retrieval date is not source freshness.

When a public map contains subject matter governed by precision/elevation, imagery or specified thematic-content clauses, read those clauses for the actual data and expression. Do not equate imagery resolution with positional accuracy, move real features arbitrarily, or claim an invented accuracy to evade an issue. Do not turn content-specific restrictions into blanket claims that ordinary river/lake outlines or every water-environment dataset are prohibited.

Source: [Public Map Content Representation Specification, 2023 document No. 2](https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/web/wjb_673085/zzjg_673183/bjhysws_674671/bhflfg/dtdmxgfl/202303/P020230313585504979937.pdf). Its original-language text controls; this page is an English workflow summary.

## Final checks and updating

Record use, extent, sources, changes, relevant provisions and unresolved questions for the final image. Keep automated QA, visual review and administrative status separate. When submission is required and authorized, find the competent authority and current guidance for the actual principal area; do not hard-code an office solely from this example.

Correct discovered source mismatches, wrong names/boundaries, missing extent or mismatched numbers before describing an image as verified for its intended public use. The 2023 document states a five-year validity period from 2023-02-06; check amendments and any continuation/replacement before relying on it, especially near or after that period. Historical snapshots are not permanent guarantees.

Standard-map portals and their files also change. Check actual editions and download formats. EPS is not automatically georeferenced GIS data, and JPG/EPS availability does not mean authoritative analysis-ready river networks or boundary coordinates are supplied. A temporary connection failure does not establish that a service has closed, moved or abandoned its rules.
